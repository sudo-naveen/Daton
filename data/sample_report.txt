Daton Annual Report 2025

Executive Summary
Daton Technologies achieved significant growth in fiscal year 2025. Total revenue reached $45.2 million, representing a 32% increase year-over-year. The company expanded its customer base to over 1,200 enterprise clients across North America and Europe.

Financial Performance
Q1 Revenue: $9.8 million
Q2 Revenue: $11.2 million
Q3 Revenue: $12.5 million
Q4 Revenue: $11.7 million

Net profit margin improved to 18.5%, up from 14.2% in 2024. Operating expenses were kept at 62% of revenue through operational efficiency improvements.

Regional Performance
North America: $28.5 million (63% of total revenue)
Europe: $12.8 million (28% of total revenue)
Asia Pacific: $3.9 million (9% of total revenue)

The North American market showed strong growth of 35%, driven by enterprise adoption of our AI analytics platform. European expansion contributed $4.2 million in new revenue.

Product Highlights
Our flagship product, Daton Analytics Platform, was adopted by 450 new enterprises. The platform processed over 2 billion data queries during 2025.

Key product achievements:
- Launched real-time analytics engine (Q2 2025)
- Released natural language query interface (Q3 2025)
- Introduced automated report generation (Q4 2025)

Employee Growth
Total headcount grew from 280 to 350 employees. Engineering team expanded by 40%. The company opened a new development center in Berlin, Germany.

Looking Ahead
For 2026, Daton targets $60 million in revenue with a focus on expanding into the Asia Pacific market. Major product launches include advanced AI capabilities and deeper database integrations.
